Two kinds of glass

Court glassLiveOnly where something IS a court: desk courts, referee courts, TV board, Sign in panel, Player Studio match court. Deep green glow, faint ball-green lines.
Data glassEverything else: tables, forms, lists, stats. In Day it is more solid (about 74%) so text stays readable over the photo. No court lines.
