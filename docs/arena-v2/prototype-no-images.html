Build notes

This file is the target look and behaviour. It is one self-contained HTML file with sample data and no backend. Build it into the existing app on rallyq-live (React + Vite, src/rallyq/*). Every name, score and photo is sample data.

1 · The three laws (from the Living Arena plan)

  • Work earns trust through certainty. Show earns attention through emotion. Match DNA connects them through truth.
  • Every spectacular treatment has one screenshot-worthy resting state and one transition. Nothing celebrates before a result is confirmed. Gold only for confirmed winners.
  • Every animation can be switched off without losing information (try Calm in the top bar).

  • Fonts: Archivo 800 tight for headlines, Archivo condensed (stretch 62%) for scores and numbers, DM Sans for body (16 px minimum on phones), Fraunces for the wordmark and the commentator line only.
  • Logo: the court is the Q, the ball is the tail. Full mark from 28 px; the no-net version at 24 px and below. Never glow, rotate, stretch or make it blue. Courts are never blue anywhere.
  • Ball green only for live dots, scores, win bars and the main action. Coral is "act now": a filled chip with dark text for things a person should do something about (Tickets on sale, 4 spots left, Needs a look, Running behind, a launch blocker). Coral is never a team colour and never on a score or game/match point. Gold only for confirmed results.
  • Night and Day are both floodlit glass (see 2c). Day matches the live site: courtside crowd photo under a Court Green tint. TV is always Night. The player's theme choice wins.
  • Club co-branding: real club logo in a chalk tile, "Hosted by [Club]", RallyQ always visible. The player and the score are always biggest.

2b · Decisions locked in the ChatGPT review

  • Day mode matches the live rallyq.in look: the courtside crowd photo under a Court Green gradient (rgba(43,110,75,.80) to rgba(24,68,45,.92)), with glass panels. The Receipt and Season Book stay printed on chalk paper in both modes.
  • Gold never appears at a game end or on any provisional score. A game end holds a chalk ring for 2.5 s. Gold appears only on a referee-confirmed winner and a confirmed champion.
  • Offline truth: an offline tap updates the pad only ("Offline · N queued"). Live, TV and the Desk freeze and say "Waiting for the court" until the server acknowledges; then every screen catches up at once.
  • Motion budget: Count runs on every score. Wash and the serve Pulse run only on the featured scoreboard (Live arena, TV main, Match day), never on court lists or cards. No looping glows, no auto count-ups on Home.
  • Sample data is always marked. Entries are requests until the club confirms place and payment.

2c · Arena System v2 (Claude + ChatGPT, approved)

  • Glass: Night fill #081E13 at 60%, blur 16 px, edge #F1F4EA at 20%, top light #F1F4EA at 10%, shadow 0 16px 48px rgba(0,0,0,.28). Day fill #081E13 at 52%, edge white 25%, top light white 12%. Work glass (referee, desk) is 85% fill with 20 px blur. At most 3 blur layers on screen; never animate blur.
  • Teams: Team A Sea Glass #76E0D0, Team B Tide Blue #7EAEF2. Ball green #D9F24A means "now" only (server, live, current run). Gold #E8C35A only after the referee confirms. Signal #BDCBC6 for stale and offline. Coral #FF655C for act-now alerts. Danger #AD2945 only for destructive actions.
  • The Q keeps score: each rally draws one stroke around the RallyQ Q, outward in Sea for Team A, inward in Tide for Team B. The ball sits with the serving team. The gold rail draws only after confirmation.
  • Athlete Cover: in Player Studio the full-bleed player photo is the panel, private and public. Name, DUPR and three stats sit on the photo. The public view shows only referee-scored results.
  • Two-Serve Turn card: a 9:16 share card when the receiving side wins two rallies against Server 1 and Server 2 in a row, then scores a run.
  • Motion budget: one hero, one echo, then silence.

4 · Two zones

Show zone (spectacular)Work zone (calm, instant)
Home, Event, Live, Results, Player Studio, Venue TV, share cardsReferee pad, Tournament Desk, Organiser overview, Admin
Floodlight states, Match DNA, Rally Rewind, gold reveal, broadcast breaksPoint Receipt with Undo, Saved / Syncing / Offline · queued, Decision Preview, Launch readiness. No dimming or celebrations near controls.

5 · Signature pieces and their data rules

  • Floodlight states: off, warming up, live, match point (spotlight narrows), confirmed final (gold). Drive them from real match state only.
  • Match DNA: one mark per rally from the referee's saved rallies, up for one team, down for the other; side-outs are short marks; the current run of 3+ is ball green. The Results sample shows one mark per point for game 3.
  • Rally Rewind: drag the DNA or the slider and the score rebuilds itself; "Jump to the turning point" finds the longest run; "Share this moment" makes a club-branded 9:16 Match Memory.
  • Win chance: always labelled an estimate. A "+12 pts" chip pulses only when it moves 10 points or more.
  • The Receipt (Player Studio v3): auto-picks the most remarkable provable moment, the player can change it; club name first, a club-issued serial, Event DNA; private coach note on the back, never published. Every claim stores its proof (Evidence Contract). Say "you and Divya", never "you". No serve stats until the pad stores the server; no kitchen stats.
  • Season Book: every Receipt becomes a numbered page; personal bests in the margin; partner spreads; Wrapped is the cover, as thick as the real season.
  • Club Record Book: feats not rankings, one holder per record, consent per record, the club can post it to WhatsApp.
  • Two sources only: Referee scored and Self-logged. Practice never touches DUPR or records. DUPR is the only rating.

5b · The one rally record (single source of truth)

Every animation, story line, DNA mark, Match Signature stroke, Receipt claim and record is derived from one list. Never keep separate display counters.

{ match_id, game, n, winner_team, outcome: point | side_out | second_server, server_team, server_no, score_before "7–5–1", score_after "8–5–1", revision, synced_at }

Undo writes a new revision; every screen re-derives from the latest revision. Hover a DNA mark in this prototype to see one record.

5c · Match Signature (ChatGPT's addition)

At a confirmed result, the rally record is drawn around the RallyQ court-line Q: one stroke per point, outward for one team, inward for the other, short for side-outs, ball green for runs of 3+, the ball at the tail. It appears on Results (tap a stroke to rewind to it), the 9:16 Match Memory and the Receipt. Plain SVG from existing data: no video, no AI, no invented shots.

5d · Component states every screen must ship

StateRule
LoadingSkeleton in the final layout; never a spinner over a score.

LiveBall-green breathing chip, "updated N s ago".
Stale (>30 s)Signal grey chip with ◷ "Last updated 90 s ago"; never says Live. It turns coral only when a person must act (admin "Needs a look").
Offline (pad)"Offline · N queued"; public screens show "Waiting for the court".
Provisional"Final · awaiting referee"; no gold, no share, no record.

Phones: test at 320 and 390 px. One hero, one primary action per screen, 44 px targets, safe-area padding, sticky score and sheets never overlap the bottom tabs.

7 · How this file works

  • Top bar: jump between journeys, switch Night/Day, Desktop/Phone, and Calm (reduced motion). Layouts respond to the frame width with container queries, so Phone mode is the real phone layout.
  • One shared match engine (pickleball doubles, side-out scoring, best of 3 to 11, win by 2) drives Home, Live, TV and the Desk. Open the Referee pad and your taps become the live score everywhere; leave it and auto-play resumes.
  • Photos are the RallyQ photo set v2 (generated, approval pending), compressed and embedded.

8 · Not in this prototype

  • Sign-in, payments, real entry forms, the Social studio editor and video. Follow live means scores, not video, until video exists.
  • Counts like page views and players are sample numbers.
